# Methods Note — G-052: Norm Asymmetry and the Parallax Lever

**Applied Categorical Physics Workbench | Troy Teno | May 2026**

---

> **Public export note:** This methods note originally contained the G-052 parallax-lever
> calibration: a norm-asymmetry logit-gap formula and an associated claim that hand-tuned
> toy-geometry parameters could be made to match T5-small's production logits.
>
> This calibration has been **retired from the public evidentiary chain** and is excluded
> from this export. Its near-perfect fit was a parameter-fitting artifact — the toy
> parameters were selected to match the S-track logits, not derived independently — so it
> is not cited as a quantitative predictor anywhere in the paper. See
> `RETIREMENTS_AND_METHOD_LESSONS.md` for the full retirement rationale.
>
> The norm asymmetry phenomenon itself (I_WALK norm larger than I_JUMP norm as a
> training-frequency artifact) is reported in the S-track experiments (S-051) and is not
> affected by this retirement.
